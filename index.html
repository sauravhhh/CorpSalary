Break-up — how the CTC is built

Employer PF and Gratuity sit inside your CTC but are not paid to you monthly — PF goes to your EPF account and gratuity is paid when you leave, normally after 5 years with the company.

What is what?

CTC

Cost to Company — everything the company spends on you in a year. It is not your salary; PF (employer share) and gratuity are hidden inside it.

Basic Pay

The fixed core of your salary. PF, gratuity and HRA are all calculated as a percentage of Basic, so a higher Basic means higher PF but lower take-home today.

HRA

House Rent Allowance — paid to cover rent. Part of it can be tax-free in the old regime if you actually pay rent and keep receipts.

Special Allowance

The balancing figure. Whatever is left in the CTC after Basic, HRA, PF, gratuity and bonus is dumped here. It is fully taxable and paid monthly.

Bonus / Variable Pay

Performance-linked pay, usually paid once or twice a year, not monthly. It is part of CTC and fully taxable in the year you receive it.

Gross Pay

Basic + HRA + Special Allowance + Bonus — your total earnings before any deduction. This is CTC minus employer PF and gratuity.

EPF (PF)

Employees' Provident Fund — 12% of Basic from you and 12% from the employer goes into your EPF account as retirement savings. Your share is cut from Gross; the employer's share is already inside CTC.

Gratuity

A loyalty payment of roughly 4.81% of Basic per year, set aside inside CTC and paid when you leave, normally after 5 years of service.

Professional Tax

A small tax charged by some states for working there — usually ₹200 a month. Delhi, Haryana and UP charge none.

Income Tax (TDS)

Tax deducted by your employer every month on your salary. In the new regime (FY 2025-26), income up to ₹12 lakh after the ₹75,000 standard deduction effectively pays no tax because of the Section 87A rebate.

In-hand Salary

Gross − PF (your share) − Professional Tax − Income Tax — the amount that actually lands in your bank account. Bonus months pay more, because the bonus comes on top that month.
